Axial-Glow

A portable light shaped around an axial structure — balancing transparency, material contrast and a warm inner glow.

01 / Concept

Light, structure
and a visible axis.

Axial-Glow turns the central lighting mechanism into the visual core of the product. A translucent body reveals the vertical structure while the soft upper shade controls the atmosphere.

Early form exploration / Axial studies
02 / Design Language

Transparent outside.
Warm inside.

The product deliberately keeps its mechanism visually present. The clear lower structure catches and refracts light, creating a changing appearance as the viewing angle moves around the lamp.

03 / Product Details

A compact object
with layered depth.

Material / Light

Refraction becomes part of the form.

The transparent lower body is not treated as a neutral shell. Its vertical geometry catches highlights and creates a layered visual rhythm beneath the warm diffuser.
